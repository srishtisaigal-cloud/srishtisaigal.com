Project 01 · Conversation Design

Mini app inside ChatGPT

A cross-border money-transfer app, built inside ChatGPT — designed so sending money home is something you can simply ask for.

By Srishti Saigal · Conversation Designer Read the full case study on Notion →

Led content design for the first cross-border money transfer app inside ChatGPT. Designed guardrails, a content design system, a decision tree, and more.

The challenge

Inside an assistant, there are no screens to lean on — the conversation is the interface. People type freely, so the system has to read intent, handle ambiguity, and never leave someone stuck in the middle of a financial task.

What I designed

I mapped the messy ways people ask into a clear set of actions, defined the money-transfer flow from request to rate estimate, wrote the guardrails that keep it accurate and on-brand, and shaped Remitly's voice for a brand-new surface.
